# Pedestrian Crossing Marking Standard

## Document Information

Client: Demo Municipal Corporation

District: District 3

Document Type: Technical Standard

Standard Reference: STD-RS-004

Version: 2.1

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

---

## Purpose

This standard defines inspection and maintenance considerations for pedestrian crossing road markings.

---

## Condition Assessment

Pedestrian crossing markings should be inspected for:

- Visibility.
- Continuity.
- Uniformity.
- Surface condition.
- Contrast with the road surface.
- Obstructions.
- Conditions affecting visibility.

---

## Degraded Markings

Where markings are significantly degraded, the responsible authority should evaluate whether corrective maintenance is required.

The evaluation should consider:

- Traffic conditions.
- Pedestrian activity.
- Visibility.
- Road geometry.
- Lighting conditions.
- Other relevant site conditions.

---

## Temporary Measures

Temporary measures may be considered when permanent maintenance cannot be completed immediately and field conditions indicate that an interim measure is appropriate.

The specific temporary measure must be selected and approved by the responsible authority.

---

## Deferral

Deferring corrective maintenance may be considered when:

1. The condition remains within acceptable operational limits.
2. A planned project provides a suitable opportunity for coordinated maintenance.
3. Required safety and technical conditions remain satisfied.
4. The responsible authority has verified the current field condition.
