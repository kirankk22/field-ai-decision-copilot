## Road Safety Maintenance

The District 3 maintenance programme includes periodic inspection and corrective maintenance of road safety assets.

Relevant activities include:

- Road marking maintenance.
- Sign maintenance.
- Street lighting maintenance.
- Safety-related roadside maintenance.

---

## Maintenance Prioritisation

Maintenance requests should be prioritised using:

1. Current condition.
2. Operational importance.
3. Safety considerations.
4. Frequency of use.
5. Availability of planned projects.
6. Cost and resource requirements.

---

## Coordination

Where corrective maintenance can reasonably be coordinated with an already planned infrastructure project, the responsible asset manager may evaluate the combined approach.

Coordination must not compromise required safety or operational conditions.

---

## Review

Assets with significant deterioration should be reviewed by the responsible maintenance authority.
