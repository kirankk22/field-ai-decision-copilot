## Project Overview

The District 3 Resurfacing Programme 2027 covers selected road sections that require pavement rehabilitation and resurfacing.

The programme is intended to combine pavement renewal with associated road-marking and minor roadside maintenance activities where practical.

## Planned Work

The planned project includes:

1. Pavement resurfacing.
2. Renewal of road surface markings where required.
3. Repainting of pedestrian crossing markings where the existing markings are degraded.
4. Review of road signs and associated markings.
5. Post-work inspection.

## Coordination Principle

Where a road-marking maintenance requirement occurs on a road section already scheduled for resurfacing, the maintenance activity should be evaluated for coordination with the resurfacing project.

Coordination may reduce duplicated mobilisation and traffic-management activities.

The responsible asset manager must verify that waiting for the planned project does not create an unacceptable safety or operational risk.

---

## Asset-Specific Note

ROAD-BBSR-102 is included within the University Road Corridor project area.

The current project planning information indicates that road-marking renewal can be considered as part of the resurfacing activity.

The actual condition of the pedestrian crossing must be verified before the final maintenance decision.

---

## Decision Considerations

Before deciding whether to repaint immediately or coordinate with the planned resurfacing project, the responsible officer should consider:

- Current condition of the crossing.
- Visibility to pedestrians and drivers.
- Traffic volume.
- Safety risk.
- Time until planned resurfacing.
- Availability of temporary measures.
- Cost of immediate intervention.
- Potential duplication of mobilisation and traffic-management costs.
